Build or refine a metric instrument model using docs/instruments/README.md.
Read repository instructions and LOCAL.md if present. Continue through geometry,
review PNGs, state meshes, URDF, validation and publishing the current package.

Use previous evidence as well as the latest capture. Preserve originals under
 dataset/instruments/<machine>/captures/<date>_<source>_<batch>/raw/.
Keep documents and review evidence under references/, state indexes under
states/, and readings in measurements.json. Catalog paths are repository-relative
in dataset/instruments/catalog.json. Ask when a ruler or feature is ambiguous.

Prefer simple convex collision pieces while retaining openings and contact
surfaces needed by the task. Keep measured dimensions, photo estimates and
unknowns distinct. Align evidence before geometric comparisons. Separate moving
parts; do not fuse open and closed states into a rigid reconstruction. Match
visible symmetry and continuity where supported by evidence. Do not invent
internal motions or precision fits from still images.

For visuals, use actual photographed panels, logos and text with recorded source
corners and UV placement. Sampled material colours are not full-surface textures.
Keep visual-only markings separate from collision geometry.

Reuse src/instruments/ components. Define dimensions, parts and joint relationships
once in the machine recipe, using design.py/shared_geometry.py for both native
OpenSCAD and convex simulation geometry. Store editable dimensions in the current
parameters.json/design_parameters with measurement evidence. Do not maintain a
second SCAD geometry recipe. To import numeric edits from parameters_native.scad:
 .venv/bin/python -m src.instruments build --model <machine> --from-cad-parameters --check-cad
Arbitrary assembly.scad edits are not imported. Engine checks require OpenSCAD.
Compare edited-parameter CAD/GLB/URDF exports in temporary validation packages;
never publish test dimensions as new measurements. CLI:
 .venv/bin/python -m src.instruments build --model <machine>
 .venv/bin/python -m src.instruments validate --model <machine>
 .venv/bin/python -m src.instruments share

Publish output/instruments/<machine>/model/ with the compact defaults: closed/open
textured GLBs, assembly PNG, combined dimension sheet, SCAD, URDF with all required
assets, parameters, provenance and check summaries. Use --extras for additional
reviews/states, --check-cad --keep-validation for retained CAD diagnostics, share
for ZIPs, and stl --model <machine> --part <semantic-part> for a printing export.
Failed diagnostics live under <machine>/validation/failures/.
Use .build_work temporarily; do not retain dated builds or backups. Preserve
small rebuild inputs under src/instruments/inputs/. Update the shared overview
at output/instruments/index.html and instruments_review.png. Report concrete
changes, checks performed and unresolved measurements with links to previews.

For new evidence, update src/instruments/inputs/<machine>/evidence.json:
 geometry_sources = original files actually used for geometry interpretation;
 materials = decoded review filenames + original paths + crop rectangles;
 markings = original source + review resize + four corners + target panel bounds.
Preserve earlier evidence unless superseded by a documented observation. Inspect
new crops/corners; they are image-specific. Do not treat adding photos as automatic
geometry fitting. For a new instrument, author and register its shared recipe;
reuse exporters, primitives, texturing, packaging and checks. Log assumptions and
measurements needed. Never describe estimates or sampled checks as physical proof.
The pipeline graph and source-release requirements are in the guide.
